All domestic United States and European Union orders dispatch via priority air cargo within 24 hours of checkout verification from our Oregon or Milan hubs. Continental shipping averages 2–3 business days. Worldwide international shipping includes fully prepaid customs clearance and direct courier handoff (DHL Express / FedEx Priority) with end-to-end telemetry tracking.

Every authentic ZYRO silhouette features an uncompromised structural integrity guarantee on the internal carbon fiber matrix plate and midsole chassis. If your sole delaminates, fractures, or registers a loss of elastic rebound within verified usage parameters, our laboratory will immediately replace or rebuild your footwear at zero cost.

Because our EcoKnit weave uses thermo-bonded polymer filaments, never machine wash or submerge in hot water above 30°C. To clean track grit or urban debris: wipe using lukewarm water and a soft bristle horsehair brush with neutral pH botanical soap. Air dry at room temperature away from direct solar radiation to preserve carbon resin elasticity.

ZYRO silhouettes are built true-to-size on our proprietary dual-gender anatomical last. If you wear custom orthotics or run between half-sizes, we recommend consulting our Concierge team prior to dispatch or selecting the half-size up for forefoot splay.
